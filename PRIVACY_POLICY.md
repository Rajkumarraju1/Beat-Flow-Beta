# Privacy Policy for OrbitMusic

**Effective Date:** March 19, 2026

OrbitMusic respects your privacy. This application is designed to work offline and does not collect personal data from users.

## 1. Information We Collect
OrbitMusic does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

## 2. Local Data Access
The app may request permission to access your device storage to:
* Detect and display audio files stored on your device
* Provide music playback functionality

All data remains on your device and is never transmitted to any server.

## 3. Lyrics Feature
If users add lyrics manually or import files, the data is stored locally on the device and is not shared externally.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Currently, OrbitMusic does not use third-party services that collect user data.

In future updates, the app may include advertising services (such as Google AdMob). These services may collect anonymized data as per their own privacy policies.

## 5. Data Security
Since all data is stored locally, users have full control over their data.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

By using OrbitMusic, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
