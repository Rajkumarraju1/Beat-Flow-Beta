BeatFlow Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 27, 2026

BeatFlow is a local music player. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Permissions

  • Audio Recording (RECORD_AUDIO): Used exclusively for real-time audio visualization. We do not record or upload any audio.
  • Media Storage: Used to scan and play your local music files.

All app operations happen locally on your device. We do not use third-party analytics.
